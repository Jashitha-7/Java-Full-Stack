css tranform:
------------
    Used to apply 2D & 3D transformations to an element.
    rotate transform:rotate(45deg);
    ->scale:
        tranform:scale(2);
        tranform:scale(0.5);
        tranform:scale(2,3);
        tranform:scaleX(0.5);
        tranform:scaleY(0.5);
    ->translate:
        tranform:translate(30px);
        tranform:translate(30px,40px);
        tranform:translateX(30px);
        tranform:translateY(30px);
    ->skew:
        tranform:skew(30deg);

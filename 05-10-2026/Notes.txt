transitions:
------------
    Transitions enable you to define the transitions between two states of an element.
    transition-property: font-size, width, etc
    transition-duration: 2s,4ms...
    transition-timing-function: ease-in/ ease-out, linear, steps,..
    transition-delay:2s,4ms,..

css tranform:
------------
    Used to apply 2D & 3D transformations to an element.
    rotate transform:rotate(45deg);
